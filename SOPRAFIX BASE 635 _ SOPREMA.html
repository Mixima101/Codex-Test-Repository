SEPD - SBS-Modified Bitumen Roofing Membrane (Torch Applied)

Description

Mechanically fastened base sheet membrane composed of a non-woven polyester reinforcement and SBS modified bitumen.
Benefits
  • The mechanical fastening system holds down the system even in high wind areas.
  • The membrane is independent of surface components and does not require insulation overlay boards, which prevents wrinkles and folds.
  • Reduced application expenses cut the system cost.
  • Made in Canada
